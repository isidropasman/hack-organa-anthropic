# ORGANA Demo Script — Hackathon Presentation

**Total time: 5 minutes**

---

## Opening (30s)

> "Every company has operational knowledge locked inside people's heads. When someone leaves, that knowledge walks out the door. ORGANA fixes that — upload your org chart, train AI agents in 10 minutes, and anyone can chat with any colleague, anytime, even after they're gone."

---

## Screen 1: Org chart upload (60s)

1. Open `/` — show the clean upload interface
2. Click **"Load Nova Agency demo"** — 13 agents appear instantly
3. Point out the grid:
   - **Green "Trained" badges** — Valentina, Martín, Sofía
   - **Gray "Untrained" badges** — the other 10
4. Readiness score bar on each card
5. Say: *"Three people have already gone through the 10-minute interview. Let me show you what that looks like."*

---

## Screen 2: Onboarding interview (90s)

1. Click on **Lucía Méndez** (untrained Account Manager)
2. ARIA introduces itself, asks first question
3. Type a realistic answer: *"I manage 4 client accounts — Banco Ciudad, Claro, Despegar, and a new retail brand. My week is mostly calls, status updates, and briefing the creative team."*
4. ARIA follows up — show 2-3 turns
5. Interrupt: *"In reality this takes 10 minutes. Let me jump to a trained agent."*

---

## Screen 3: Chat with trained agent (120s)

1. Click on **Valentina Torres** (CEO, trained)
2. Ask: *"Valentina, what's your rule for when to escalate a client issue to you directly?"*
3. Show the specific, knowledge-base-grounded response (mentions Natura, the 2-hour rule, etc.)
4. Ask: *"How do you handle a pitch that needs to go out fast?"*
5. Show response referencing the 72-hour rule
6. Switch to **Sofía Chen** (Head of Creative)
7. Ask: *"Sofía, a client is on round 3 of feedback on a design. What do I do?"*
8. Show response about the change order policy

---

## Contrast moment (30s)

Click on any untrained agent, go to their chat page (redirect to onboard).
Say: *"Without training, agents redirect you to start the interview. The difference is night and day."*

---

## Closing (30s)

> "10 minutes per employee. Every process, every tool, every decision pattern — captured. ORGANA doesn't replace people. It preserves what makes them irreplaceable."

---

## Backup questions / judges

**"Why not just write a wiki?"**
> People don't write wikis. They answer questions. ORGANA works the way people actually communicate.

**"What happens when knowledge is outdated?"**
> Agents can be re-trained in another 10-minute interview. Think of it like a quarterly review.

**"Why Claude specifically?"**
> The quality of the prompts is the product. Claude's instruction-following and nuance in role-play makes the agents feel real, not generic.
